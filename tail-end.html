The bar is your whole lifetime of in-person days together. The grey part is already behind you.

This is the point of the article: the number feels endless, but you can count it.

What can change the numbers

Same life expectancy. Only your choices change.

Assumptions: you lived with your parents every day until you left home; past visits followed the same rhythm as today. A rough model, not a prediction.
